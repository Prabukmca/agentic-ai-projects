# Unauthorized Transaction Reporting (Curated)

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Guidance for dispute and fraud reporting timelines.

Key points:
- Customers should report unauthorized transactions as soon as detected.
- Early reporting may reduce customer liability under bank policy/regulatory rules.
- Banks may require a complaint reference and basic incident details.
- Complex liability or disputed outcomes should be escalated to human support.
