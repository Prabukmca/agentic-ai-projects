# Home Loan Process (Curated)

Source: https://www.icicibank.com/loans/home-loan
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Home loan eligibility and application guidance.

Key points:
- Home loans are typically secured against the property being purchased or constructed.
- Eligibility depends on income, credit history, employment type, property value, and repayment capacity.
- Lenders usually assess loan-to-value (LTV) ratio and the borrower’s eligible EMI affordability.
- Common required documents include identity proof, address proof, income proof, property documents, and bank statements.
- The process generally includes application, document verification, property evaluation, sanction, and disbursement.
- Borrowers should compare interest rate type, tenure, processing fees, and prepayment conditions before finalizing.
- Some banks require co-borrowers or guarantors depending on the borrower profile and property type.
- Disbursement may happen in a single tranche or staggered tranches depending on project construction stage.
