# Digital Banking Safe Practices (Curated)

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: General security practices for online/mobile banking support.

Key points:
- Use official apps and verified websites only.
- Do not click unknown links in SMS/email that request account verification.
- Enable transaction alerts and regularly review account activity.
- Report suspicious activity to bank support immediately.
