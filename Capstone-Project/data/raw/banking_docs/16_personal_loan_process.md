# Personal Loan Process (Curated)

Source: https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal-loans
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Personal loan eligibility and application guidance.

Key points:
- Personal loans are generally unsecured loans that depend on income, credit profile, repayment capacity, and employment stability.
- Banks usually ask for identity proof, address proof, PAN, income proof, bank statements, and employment details.
- Eligibility is often based on age, monthly income, existing EMIs, and credit score.
- Applicants can apply online, through branch channels, or through pre-approved offers.
- Loan approval timelines vary by lender, document completeness, and verification checks.
- Borrowers should confirm the sanctioned amount, interest rate, tenure, processing fee, and repayment schedule before accepting.
- Missing or inconsistent documents can delay sanction or lead to rejection.
- Customers should review the terms around prepayment, foreclosure, and late payment before signing.
