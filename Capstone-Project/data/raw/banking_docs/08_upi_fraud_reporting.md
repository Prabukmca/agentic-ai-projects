# UPI and Instant-Payment Fraud Reporting (Curated)

Source: https://www.npci.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Fraud escalation playbook for digital payments.

Key points:
- Suspected payment fraud should be reported immediately through official bank and payment channels.
- Users should preserve transaction references, screenshots, and timestamps.
- Agent should avoid promising reversals and instead guide users to rapid reporting channels.
- OTP/PIN sharing requests indicate high risk and require escalation.
