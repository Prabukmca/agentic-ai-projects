# Foreign Money Transfer Charges (Curated)

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: International transfer cost and fee explanation.

Key points:
- Foreign outward remittance charges typically include transfer fees, exchange rate spread, and sometimes tax or GST.
- Banks may apply a fixed fee or a percentage-based fee depending on the transfer amount and destination.
- Exchange rate conversion can be more significant than the visible fee because the bank may add a spread to the interbank rate.
- For international transfers, receiving banks may also charge a receiving fee or intermediary processing fee.
- Transfer limits may be lower for certain remittance purposes such as education, travel, family maintenance, or investment transactions.
- Customers should validate the total outflow amount before sending funds internationally.
- Some payment methods are faster but more expensive; wire transfers and specialist remittance channels may differ in cost and delivery time.
- Banks usually require purpose codes and supporting documents for outward remittances above certain thresholds or for specific categories.
