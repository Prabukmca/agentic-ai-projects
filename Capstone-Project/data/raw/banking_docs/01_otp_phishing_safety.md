# OTP and Phishing Safety (Curated)

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Safety guidance for fraud-risk prompts.

Key points:
- Banks should never ask customers to share OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords on calls or messages.
- If a caller asks for one-time codes, treat it as potential fraud.
- Customers should terminate the call and contact official bank support using verified channels.
- Immediate escalation is recommended when account misuse is suspected.
