# Online Transfer Limits (Curated)

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Daily transfer caps and transaction rules.

Key points:
- Online transfer limits vary based on channel, account type, beneficiary type, and bank policy.
- NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, and card-based transfers may have separate transaction caps.
- Some banks allow higher limits after account verification, device binding, or risk profile review.
- Daily transaction ceilings often apply to outgoing credit transfers, international transfers, and wallet top-ups.
- Beneficiary additions or first-time payments may require additional verification or time delays.
- Customers should confirm the exact limit in the app prior to initiating a high-value transfer.
- If the transfer limit is exceeded, customers may need to split the payment or use alternate transfer methods.
- Apps and net banking portals usually show the remaining limit for the day after a transfer is initiated.
