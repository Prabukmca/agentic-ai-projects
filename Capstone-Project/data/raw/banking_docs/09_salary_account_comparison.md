# Salary Account Comparison Checklist (Curated)

Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/
Last-checked: 2026-09-30
Use: Structured advisory for low-fee account selection.

Key points:
- Compare monthly balance requirement, service charges, debit card fees, and ATM access.
- Compare branch/network accessibility and digital service quality.
- Confirm hidden charges such as SMS alerts or replacement-card costs.
- For final selection, users should compare current fee schedules on official bank pages.
